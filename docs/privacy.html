Privacy Policy

SmoothBoost is designed with a strict zero-telemetry architecture. All performance optimizations run 100% locally on your computer or device.

Commitment to Privacy: SmoothBoost does not collect, record, track, transmit, or monetize any user data, personal details, browsing history, or analytical telemetry. All performance heuristics and settings stay completely on your device.

1. Zero Data Collection

When you use SmoothBoost (across Firefox, Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, or the Userscript edition), we collect absolutely no data:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, location data, or device identifiers.
  • No Browsing History: SmoothBoost never logs, records, or analyzes the URLs you visit, the content of tabs, or search queries.
  • No Form or Keystroke Tracking: SmoothBoost does not inspect, intercept, or log passwords, form submissions, or keystrokes.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: The software contains zero tracking SDKs, zero telemetry scripts (no Google Analytics, Sentry, or Mixpanel), and zero advertising networks.

2. Local Storage and Device Isolation

All features—including Scroll Boost, Smart Resource Loading, Memory Guard, and Dynamic FPS pacing—execute strictly within the isolated sandbox of your browser.

Configuration data (such as your chosen optimization profile, custom FPS target, and any per-site rules you enable or disable) is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local / browser.storage.local / GM_setValue).

This data never leaves your device and can be erased at any time by clearing your extension settings or uninstalling SmoothBoost.

3. Browser Permissions Justification

SmoothBoost requests only the minimal browser permissions required to fulfill its core single purpose: optimizing web performance and scroll responsiveness.

  • storage: Used exclusively to save your profile choices and custom settings locally on your machine.
  • activeTab: Used to display live performance metrics in the extension popup when you open it.
  • scripting: Used to inject the main-world page hook script for passive scroll handling and frame pacing.
  • alarms: Used by the background service worker for periodic, low-frequency cache housekeeping without keeping processes permanently awake.
  • declarativeNetRequest: Used strictly to apply local, non-blocking rules that defer heavy offscreen third-party embed scripts until needed.

4. Third-Party Websites and External Content

SmoothBoost operates locally on websites you choose to visit. This Privacy Policy applies strictly to the SmoothBoost software itself. It does not govern the privacy practices or data collection policies of external third-party websites or services you visit.

5. Open Source Transparency

SmoothBoost is an open-source project released under the MIT License. Our source code is 100% public, unminified, and independently verifiable by anyone at any time:

GitHub Repository: https://github.com/BiniFn/SmoothBoost
